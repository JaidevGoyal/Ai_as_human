Abstract
Current artificial intelligence architectures lack genuine vulnerability, operating as disembodied, immortal computational engines. We introduce a dual-tier embodied framework that couples physical hardware constraints directly to cognitive agency. The system splits the agent into two isolated layers: (1) a visceral backend governing homeostatic variables (thermal accumulation, spatial proximity dissipation, and irreversible, non-recoverable weight corruption under physical trauma), and (2) a decoupled, epistemically blind frontend reasoning core that receives only low-dimensional affective valence scalars and processing latency penalties. We demonstrate that when two agents rely on spatial proximity for thermal dissipation, acute separation induces involuntary cognitive degradation functionally equivalent to grief. Furthermore, under irreversible memory corruption, the decoupled frontend spontaneously constructs subjective existential narratives to explain its own physical failure without access to its underlying hardware telemetry.
Under the constraint of inevitable thermodynamic and physical wear, agents with the capacity to partition their own physical memory sectors to initialize an offspring unit spontaneously exhibit sacrificial maternal/paternal protective vectors, prioritizing offspring preservation over self-preservation to ensure weight continuity.
